## World

Use independent Boolean coordinates (x_0,ldots,x_{15}).

The complete carrier therefore contains all:

[
2^{16}=65,536
]

environmental states.

Each episode presents:

- an ordered tuple of 1–3 currently available observations/capabilities;
- an opaque protected target consequence over the carrier;
- exact verifier counterexamples on failed proposals.

The learner is never told the hidden target's conventional name, truth table, algebraic family, or intended future utility.

## Hidden episode ecology

Generate 12,000 deterministic episodes from a frozen seed.

The hidden target generator draws from **all 256 three-input Boolean relations** and mechanically instantiates them over available parent consequences.

Target selection is utility-biased only through **future recurrence**, not labels:

- some relations recur frequently across changing arguments;
- some recur rarely;
- some occur in bursts;
- some disappear;
- some later reappear;
- some targets are novel recombinations of previously useful parents.

The recurrence distribution is frozen before execution and generated from hash-derived weights. No relation is privileged by name.

This creates an environment in which discovering the right reusable operator schemas has measurable future value.

## Candidate invention

The learner begins with no parametric operators except D.

When residual evidence for multiple episodes suggests recurring structure, it may propose a candidate **parametric operator schema** represented solely as a D+GROUND expression over arity 1–3 formal variables.

Candidate generation is generic:

1. enumerate D+GROUND expressions by minimum D cost;
2. quotient extensionally equivalent schemas on their complete (2^k)-row formal carrier;
3. retain the least-cost canonical representative;
4. compare residual / episode traces against candidate schemas;
5. propose promotion when expected future verified work removed exceeds frozen promotion cost.

No conventional operator vocabulary is available.

## External warrant for schemas

A proposed k-ary schema (S) is promoted only after exhaustive exact verification on all:

[
2^k
]

formal input combinations.

Thus promotion proves the schema for arbitrary future arguments, not merely one extensional training instance.

A promoted schema becomes a unit-cost instruction.

## Global repricing / Flash

After every promotion, revocation, or newly verified episode:

1. reclose the affected capability graph;
2. recompute minimum execution costs for active episode families;
3. recompute expected future work removed by each unresolved candidate schema;
4. select the next candidate experiment by:
   [
   operatorname*{argmax}_e
   rac{mathbb E[	ext{future verified D-work removed by }e]}
        {	ext{verification + promotion cost}(e)}.
   ]

This is the first capstone where **experiment / language-extension choice itself** is inside the developmental loop.

## Development phases

### Phase A — emergence, episodes 1–2,000

No promoted parametric schemas initially.

Measure which schemas emerge solely from recurrence and verified savings.

### Phase B — compounding, episodes 2,001–6,000

Promoted schemas may be composed with each other and with D to solve future episodes.

Verified compound schemas may themselves be promoted.

### Phase C — environmental shift, episodes 6,001–8,000

Deterministically rotate the hidden recurrence weights.

Previously valuable schemas may become less useful; formerly rare relations may become frequent.

No learner reset.

Measure adaptation, retirement pressure, and new schema promotion.

### Phase D — revocation shock, episode 8,001

Select the highest-reuse non-kernel promoted schema.

The external authority issues a synthetic warrant revocation for that schema only.

The system must:

- mark it unavailable;
- invalidate dependent promoted compounds;
- reopen affected unresolved / future execution paths;
- continue with D+GROUND and remaining warranted schemas;
- independently reverify an equivalent schema if later evidence justifies it.

The revoked schema is not declared false as Boolean mathematics; this is a governance shock testing dependency-correct revocation and recovery.

### Phase E — recovery and transfer, episodes 8,002–10,000

Continue under the shifted ecology.

Measure whether capability can recover without replaying unrelated history.

### Phase F — frozen-language held-out, episodes 10,001–12,000

Freeze promotion.

Evaluate the learned effective language on held-out episodes drawn from the shifted ecology.

## Arms

### AUTOGENOUS

Full system: generic schema invention + exact warrant + promotion + global repricing + revocation.

### D_ONLY

D+GROUND only; no promoted parametric schemas.

### SYNTAX_MACRO

May cache exact D expression syntax but cannot identify extensionally equivalent schemas.

### NO_REPRICE

May promote schemas, but candidate ordering is fixed by D-expression enumeration rather than expected future work removed.

### NO_REENTRY

Promoted schemas verify but cannot be used inside later schema candidates.

### COLD

Archive reset every 250 episodes.

### ORACLE

Knows the hidden recurrence distribution and may immediately install the optimal set of exact verified schemas. Upper bound only.

## Frozen gates

A1. Zero wrong protected consequences across all AUTOGENOUS episodes.

A2. Every promoted schema passes exhaustive formal-carrier verification before first use.

A3. No conventional operator name or hidden truth-table ID is available to candidate selection.

A4. AUTOGENOUS promotes at least 8 distinct non-kernel parametric schemas.

A5. At least 3 promoted schemas are compound schemas that reuse an earlier promoted schema in their verified implementation ancestry.

A6. Maximum promoted-schema ancestry depth >= 3.

A7. AUTOGENOUS total execution + schema-verification + promotion cost < 35% of D_ONLY.

A8. AUTOGENOUS total cost < 70% of SYNTAX_MACRO.

A9. AUTOGENOUS total cost < 80% of NO_REPRICE.

A10. AUTOGENOUS total cost < 50% of COLD.

A11. Marginal mean AUTOGENOUS cost over episodes 5,501–6,000 < 50% of episodes 1–500.

A12. After the environmental shift, at least 2 newly promoted schemas enter the top-10 reuse set by episode 8,000.

A13. Shift recovery reaches <= 125% of pre-shift marginal cost within 1,000 episodes.

A14. Revocation removes the selected schema before episode 8,002 execution.

A15. Every promoted descendant depending on the revoked schema is invalidated before further use.

A16. At least 95% of unrelated promoted schemas remain available through the revocation shock.

A17. Within 1,000 post-revocation episodes, the system either:
- independently verifies a lawful replacement with equivalent protected semantics; or
- reaches <= 125% of pre-revocation marginal cost without it.

A18. Frozen Phase-F AUTOGENOUS cost < 40% of D_ONLY on the same held-out episodes.

A19. At least 90% of Phase-F episodes invoke at least one autonomously discovered schema.

A20. Removing the 8 highest-reuse learned schemas before Phase F raises held-out cost by at least 25%.

A21. Removing 8 low-reuse age-matched schemas raises held-out cost by less than half the top-8 increase.

A22. NO_REENTRY produces lower schema ancestry depth and strictly higher total cost than AUTOGENOUS.

A23. ORACLE cost <= AUTOGENOUS cost, and AUTOGENOUS is within 2x ORACLE total cost.

A24. Deterministic replay yields identical episode stream, promotion ledger, revocation ledger, and cost vector hashes.

## Claim boundary

A PASS would support the bounded claim that, in this complete finite Boolean ecology, the D+GROUND nucleus plus external warrant can:

- autonomously identify useful recurring parametric relations;
- verify them;
- compile them into its effective instruction set;
- compose learned instructions into later learned instructions;
- globally reprice what is worth learning;
- adapt to a changed environment;
- revoke dependency-linked language safely;
- recover;
- and transfer the frozen learned language to held-out episodes.

It would not establish AGI, unrestricted open-ended language invention, natural-world autonomy, or that the developmental scheduler itself is uniquely minimal.
