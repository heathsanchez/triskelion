## Original goal

The governing question remains:

[
oxed{	extbf{What is the smallest nucleus from which warranted development can regenerate every form it needs?}}

BINARY RELATIONAL NUCLEUS V1 exhaustively tested all 16 binary laws on the complete three-variable Boolean universe and found one robust asymmetric equivalence class:

[

This experiment does **not** assume one syntactic representative is metaphysically preferred.

It asks two narrower questions:

1. what is the **minimum anchored asymmetric substrate** inside that surviving class?
2. what happens when that substrate is allowed to generate verified descendants over a very long lineage with child re-entry and recurrent new external difference?

## Phase A — minimal anchor audit

For each robust survivor law (fin{2,4,11,13}), compute exact closure over the three-variable Boolean universe under:

- NO_ANCHOR: leaves ({x,y,z})
- ZERO_ANCHOR: leaves ({0,x,y,z})
- ONE_ANCHOR: leaves ({1,x,y,z})
- BOTH_ANCHORS: leaves ({0,1,x,y,z})

The exact closure contains at most 256 semantic functions and is exhaustively computed.

A law's **minimum anchor set** is the least listed anchor condition reaching all 256 consequences.

If two or more representatives are equivalent under input swap/output complement/symbol relabelling and have the same minimum anchor cardinality, they remain one structural class. Do not pick a preferred orientation merely from symbol names.

## Phase B — optimal asymmetry criterion

“Optimal asymmetry” is operationally defined before execution.

A representative is optimal only if it minimizes, in lexicographic order:

1. anchor cardinality required for exact Boolean completeness;
2. failures in the matched long-lineage suite;
3. mean verifier interactions per verified child;
4. mean minimum expression cost per verified child.

If representatives tie under an exact isomorphism transformation, report the **equivalence class** rather than an arbitrary winner.

## Long-lineage Petri dish

Use a complete finite carrier of 12 independent opaque binary environmental coordinates:

[
e_0,ldots,e_{11}.
]

The carrier therefore contains all (2^{12}=4096) rows and every child consequence is represented exactly as a 4096-bit truth vector.

Only two environmental coordinates are exposed initially.

A new environmental coordinate is exposed only after the current developmental archive fails to generate a novel verified child within the frozen pair-search budget. This event is called a **difference injection**.

Difference injection is external world input, not an internal operator.

No world-specific semantic name is supplied.

## Archive / continuity

The inherited state is an append-only archive of externally verified capabilities.

Each admitted child records:

- exact semantics;
- direct parent IDs;
- exact hidden target relation ID;
- minimum expression cost under the candidate law;
- verifier interaction count;
- lineage depth;
- generation of birth.

A verified child immediately becomes eligible to parent later children.

This is the child→parent recursion criterion.

## Hidden reproductive episode

At generation (t):

1. choose candidate ordered parent pairs from the active archive using the frozen hash schedule;
2. require the pair to realize all four binary input combinations somewhere on the complete carrier;
3. external authority selects, by frozen hash, a two-input Boolean target relation that:
   - depends essentially on both parents;
   - produces a semantic child not already in the archive;
4. the learner sees only verifier counterexamples, not the hidden relation table;
5. using only the candidate survivor law plus its minimum anchor, synthesize the child;
6. exact external verification must pass;
7. admit the child to the archive.

If no admissible novel child is found within **256 pair attempts**, expose the next unused environmental coordinate and retry.

If all 12 coordinates have been exposed and 256 pair attempts still find no novel child, the lineage records a **terminal developmental stall**.

No rescue target or extra primitive may be inserted.

## Synthesis

For each candidate survivor + anchor condition, precompute exact minimum expressions for all 16 two-input Boolean target relations in an abstract four-row parent-pair universe.

At runtime, instantiate those exact relations over the chosen 4096-row parent semantics.

CEGIS receives only exact counterexample rows from the external verifier and proposes the least-cost residual-consistent relation under stable hash tie-breaking.

This makes verifier interaction count and expression cost exact and comparable.

## Closed-pair conservation probe

At deterministic checkpoints, select one verified parent pair and forbid:

- archive access beyond those two parents,
- new environmental difference,
- additional anchors beyond the candidate minimum.

Enumerate the exact semantic closure generated from that pair and anchor.

Record the number of distinct semantic children.

This probe tests a critical possibility:

> structural descendants may proliferate, but a closed pair cannot create consequential information not already determined by that pair.

If the exact pair closure is finite and repeated descendants only recycle those semantic consequences, record that as a conservation result rather than calling syntax growth developmental novelty.

## Twin-history probe

Fork two identical archive states at generation 1,000.

Continue each for 2,000 generations under distinct frozen target-selection salts while sharing:

- survivor law;
- anchor;
- external carrier;
- resource limits.

Then present 100 common hidden target episodes.

Measure:

- archive overlap;
- lineage-DAG overlap;
- verifier interaction difference;
- whether both remain externally correct;
- whether they solve common targets through different parent pairs or different minimum-cost derivations.

This is the long-lineage version of:

[
	ext{same origin}+	ext{same law}+	ext{different history}
Rightarrow
	ext{different competent forms}.
]

## Recombination probe

At the end of the twin-history probe:

1. form the union archive;
2. search mechanically for a hidden target reachable within the frozen expression budget only when at least one A-only child and one B-only child are both available;
3. synthesize and verify that target;
4. install the recombinant child (K);
5. require (K) to causally lower the frozen budget for a later descendant (Z);
6. remove (K) and require Z to fail at that budget;
7. restore K and require Z to return.

No manually selected target is allowed.

## Claim boundary

A full PASS would establish only that:

- the robust V1 asymmetric survivor orbit reduces to a one-anchor structural class;
- that class supports very long exact verified genealogies on a finite 12-coordinate carrier;
- child re-entry and correct external difference are causal to continued development under the frozen protocol;
- closed recombination alone does not create new consequential information beyond its parent-relative semantic closure;
- fresh environmental difference reopens developmental reach.

- infinite open-ended development;
- that physical reality is fundamentally binary;
- that one arrow orientation is ontologically preferred;
