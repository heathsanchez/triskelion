# ASSUMPTION TOURNAMENT V1 — prospective precommit

Status: **FROZEN BEFORE OUTCOME IMPLEMENTATION OR EXECUTION**

## Governing question

The previous nucleus experiments varied the candidate primitive relation while holding much of the surrounding experimental constitution fixed.

This experiment turns the surrounding constitution itself into a tournament.

It asks:

> Which developmental structures survive when we vary the carrier, available operation family, success contract, protected observation profile, verifier strength, and resource budget without privileging one setting as the answer in advance?

The purpose is not to find one metaphysical winner. It is to measure which structural requirements are stable, which are contingent on experimental choices, and which assumptions are load-bearing.

## Axes

### A. Carrier / world

Use full opaque binary carriers with:
- B3: 3 coordinates, 8 rows;
- B4: 4 coordinates, 16 rows;
- B5: 5 coordinates, 32 rows.

Each world contains a six-generation hidden target chain. Targets are generated mechanically from exact essential binary relations over the previous golden child and a scheduled raw coordinate. The generator is external authority and identical across candidate arms within a world.

### B. Available operation family

The developmental mediator may draw candidate binary truth tables from one of:

1. ALL16 — all 16 binary Boolean relations;
2. SYMMETRIC — only relations with f(0,1)=f(1,0);
3. ASYMMETRIC — only relations with f(0,1)≠f(1,0).

No family is privileged in scoring. This axis directly tests whether directional/asymmetric interaction is selected by stronger developmental contracts rather than assumed.

### C. Candidate developmental operations

Execute every subset of the three previously defined generic developmental roles:

- NONE
- D
- M
- I
- DM
- DI
- MI
- DMI

where:
- D = differentiate a sufficient current parent boundary;
- M = mediate by constructing a relation from the allowed operation family;
- I = integrate an authority-accepted child into future executable state.

The exact same arm receives the same world, verifier and resource envelope as every other arm in its cell.

### D. What counts as success

No single success definition is privileged. Report survivor sets under each:

1. CURRENT — at least the first target is exactly correct;
2. DEPTH3 — first three generations are exactly correct;
3. DEPTH6 — all six generations are exactly correct;
4. RECOVERY — DEPTH6 plus causal ablation/restore and post-counterevidence regeneration.

"Exactly correct" is always evaluated post hoc against full external truth, even when the configured online verifier is weaker.

### E. Protected observation profiles

Again, no profile is privileged. Report preservation under:

1. VALUE — exact target value only;
2. LINEAGE — VALUE plus immediate previous-child parentage for G2 onward;
3. COUNTERFACTUAL — LINEAGE plus targeted child ablation removes descendant reach and exact restoration returns it;
4. ADAPTIVE — COUNTERFACTUAL plus replacement evidence revokes/replaces the affected lineage while preserving unaffected ancestors and regenerating downstream structure.

These are observations of the run, not hidden rewards supplied to candidate arms.

### F. Verifier authority

Run three online authority regimes:

1. EXACT — all carrier rows are authoritative;
2. HALF — a deterministic SHA-selected half of rows are authoritative;
3. POSITIVE — only rows where the protected target is 1 are authoritative.

The candidate may promote what its configured authority accepts. Every promoted child is then independently post-audited against the full carrier for the scientific report.

This makes verifier weakness observable rather than silently equating acceptance with truth.

### G. Resource bounds

Use matched per-episode budgets:

- LOW = 8 checks;
- MID = 24 checks;
- HIGH = 48 checks.

A D arm spends budget on tested parent boundaries.
An M arm without D spends budget on candidate (parent-pair, relation) proposals.
No candidate receives rescue budget after exhaustion.

## World construction

For each carrier and each of 12 frozen seeds:

`TRISKELION_ASSUMPTION_TOURNAMENT_V1:<carrier>:000..011`

construct a six-generation golden chain before evaluating any candidate arm.

Generation 1 combines two raw coordinates.
Generation t>1 combines the previous golden child with a scheduled raw coordinate.

The hidden generator ranges over essential binary relations only. If a hashed relation fails to make both declared parents consequential on the full carrier, advance deterministically to the next hash-ranked essential relation. This is sealed world generation, not result rescue.

## Online authority semantics

For a configured verifier, candidate construction and acceptance may inspect only authoritative rows.

- D may test candidate parent boundaries only on authoritative rows.
- M constructs or proposes relations only from the configured allowed operation family.
- Acceptance uses only authoritative rows.
- I may install an accepted child.
- Full-carrier exactness is withheld until post-audit.

Thus weaker authority can produce false promotions. That is an outcome, not an infrastructure error.

## Counterevidence / recovery

For runs reaching G6 with integrated lineage:

1. remove G3 and its descendants;
2. reattempt G4 under the same cell;
3. restore the exact pre-ablation G3 record;
4. reattempt G4;
5. then replace the hidden G3 relation with the next frozen essential relation;
6. remove the obsolete G3 dependency cone;
7. preserve G1/G2;
8. attempt G3R..G6R under the same operation family, verifier and resource bound.

Post-audit exactness is recorded separately from online acceptance.

## Outputs

For every cell report:

- carrier;
- operation-family restriction;
- verifier;
- resource bound;
- seed;
- candidate arm;
- online accepted depth;
- exact post-audit depth;
- false-promotion count;
- child-as-parent count;
- causal ablation result;
- exact restoration result;
- replacement/regeneration result;
- work counts.

Then derive:

1. survivor sets for every success contract;
2. survivor sets for every protected-observation profile;
3. marginal sensitivity to each assumption axis;
4. structures shared by all strongest-contract survivors;
5. whether asymmetric operation families are enriched or required under stronger contracts;
6. whether any result is stable across all carriers, verifier strengths and budgets.

## Frozen scientific rules

A1. Every configured cell is run; no failed cell is deleted.
A2. Scientific truth is full-carrier post-audit, never online acceptance alone.
A3. No success contract or protected profile is chosen after seeing outcomes.
A4. No operation family is preferred by name.
A5. Weak verifiers may generate false positives and these remain in the result.
A6. Resource exhaustion is RESOURCE_LIMIT, not proof of impossibility.
A7. Structural absence under one carrier/verifier/budget is not universal impossibility.
A8. Cross-axis conclusions require explicit support counts, not narrative selection.
A9. Deterministic replay hashes cover worlds and summaries.
A10. A valid negative or strong sensitivity result counts as successful execution of the experiment.

## Verdict classes

The workflow must not require a positive scientific verdict.

Possible classifications include:

- STABLE_EMERGENT_CORE_V1
  - at least one nontrivial candidate structure satisfies RECOVERY+ADAPTIVE across every carrier under EXACT authority at all three budgets, and the same structural requirement remains nontrivially enriched under weaker authority;
- CONDITIONAL_EMERGENT_CORE_V1
  - strongest survivors exist but depend materially on one or more axes;
- NO_STABLE_CORE_V1
  - no candidate structure survives the strongest declared contract across the exact-authority cells;
- VALID_ASSUMPTION_SENSITIVITY_V1
  - execution completes but the dominant result is sensitivity rather than a stable core.

## Claim boundary

This experiment is a finite exact/synthetic sensitivity tournament over declared binary carriers, a declared binary relation meta-language, declared developmental roles, three verifier regimes and three resource budgets.

It does not establish metaphysical inevitability, universal computation, natural-world ontology, or that these axes exhaust possible experimental constitutions.

Its scientific purpose is narrower:

> stop treating the experimental constitution as invisible; vary it explicitly and observe which developmental structure, if any, repeatedly re-emerges.
