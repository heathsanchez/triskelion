# V135 — Developmental Quotient Capstone precommit

Date frozen: 2026-08-16 NZST

## Purpose

V135 is a single prospective battery aimed at the ten unresolved questions left by V103–V134. It is deliberately allowed to return a vector of PASS / FAIL / OPEN / INVALID outcomes. **No omnibus headline PASS is permitted unless every gate is independently closed by the frozen evidence.**

This experiment does not alter or consume protected information from V134. The constructor-development gates below may only consume later, independently completed V134-successor artifacts. V135 therefore cannot manufacture a constructor result to make itself pass.

## Frozen questions

## Stratum A — exact large-N quotient stability

Use the exact 3-input Boolean synthesis world of V103. Old language is variables, constants and XOR. Candidate new binary operators are all non-affine Boolean binary functions. Old-language equivalence is generated by argument swap, independent input negation and output negation.

Freeze 100 independent streams with seeds `202608160000 + i` for `i=0..99`. For each stream:

1. sample an acquisition hidden non-affine operator;
2. sample a distinct held-out hidden representative from its old-language orbit;
3. generate 30 acquisition targets at depth 3 and 50 held-out targets at depth 4, with held-out variable permutation;
4. score every non-affine candidate under the same fixed governed package charge used by V103;
5. record the exact old closure, acquisition winners, held-out winners, warm/cold governed cost and exact semantic-state expansion counts.

A1 passes iff every stream has the exact affine old closure and all held-out targets lie outside it.

A2 passes iff every acquisition-selected winner lies in the same old-language orbit as the stream's hidden capability. This is a quotient-level identity test, not a claim that the same literal representative must win.

A3 reports, but does not require, literal representative competitiveness: fraction of streams where the best acquisition winner is within 10% of the best held-out literal representative.

A4 passes iff warm retained governed cost beats cold per-target reconstruction on at least 95/100 streams, using the pre-frozen cost accounting.

A5 passes iff exact warm search expansion count beats cold reconstruction by at least 4x on at least 95/100 streams.

## Stratum B — systematic natural-code quotient induction

External corpus: QuixBugs Python, pinned commit `4257f44b0ff1181dedaedee6a447e133219fcebf`.

The task set is **not** the historical V108 list. Enumerate every single-comparator `<` or `>` AST site in every Python file under `correct_python_programs/`, excluding test/support files only by directory location. Program and site ordering are lexical / AST traversal order and frozen by this rule.

For every site, execute the unmodified upstream per-program test. Then qualify two causal mutation families independently:

- Family RELAX: `< -> <=`, `> -> >=`.
- Family REVERSE: `< -> >`, `> -> <`.

A site/family qualifies only if the unmodified source passes and the mutation fails with a nonempty exact pytest FAILED-node-ID signature. No source is selected because a candidate repair later succeeds.

### Generic relation grammar

For relation induction, use the same anonymous local comparison relation grammar as V108: operand coordinate KEEP/SWAP and strict/relaxed comparator tokens drawn from `<,>,<=,>=,==,!=`, with literal identity excluded. Selection sees only acquisition-program verifier outcomes and exact failure signatures.

For each whole-program holdout fold containing qualified RELAX evidence, score every relation candidate only on qualified RELAX sites from all other programs. A perfect relation must map every acquisition strict presentation to a passing program and every corresponding relaxed presentation to the exact acquisition mutation failure signature. Held-out evidence is not opened until selection is frozen.

B1 scale passes iff at least 20 qualified RELAX sites across at least 8 source programs are obtained prospectively. If the pinned corpus has fewer, verdict is `CORPUS_CEILING`, not a scientific failure.

B2 induction passes iff each evaluable fold has exactly one acquisition-perfect relation and at least 90% of the candidate grammar is rejected in every fold.

B3 held-out quotient transfer passes iff the selected fold relation repairs at least 90% of qualified held-out RELAX sites while literal acquisition-coordinate repair solves at most 10%, and targeted ablation returns those sites to their qualified failing mutation.

B4 cross-family reuse passes iff, without using REVERSE outcomes for relation selection, the relation selected from RELAX transports the correct REVERSE repair on at least 90% of qualified REVERSE held-out sites in evaluable source-held-out folds.

B5 representative/presentation symmetry passes iff naturally authored `<` and `>` qualified sites are both present in at least 3 source programs and relation transport succeeds at >=90% in both orientation directions under source separation. Otherwise report corpus ceiling.

## Strata F–H — higher developmental gates

These gates explicitly prevent V135 from answering open-ended questions by repackaging historical evidence.

F1 natural multi-generation compounding closes only with a fresh external sequential lineage where `O2` is not discoverable from `A0` under the frozen protocol, becomes discoverable from `A0+O1`, and ancestor ablation removes discovery of O2. Historical V54 is precedent only.

G1 constructor growth closes only from independently completed post-V134 evidence showing a fresh natural/source-distinct constructor transition and a later constructor/capability whose discoverability causally depends on that transition. V132 is precedent only.

H1 repeated/open-ended operation closes only if one frozen controller processes at least 20 independently authored sequential episodes containing at least two admitted developmental changes, one rejected/revoked change, persistence/reload, and later causal reuse, without analyst selection of the next conceptual hypothesis. Historical separate experiments do not count.

If F1/G1/H1 lack fresh qualifying artifacts at V135 execution, status is `OPEN_NOT_CLOSED`, not FAIL.
