# GOVERNED_AMORTIZATION_V1 — prospective precommit

## Prior result

GOVERNED_COMPRESSION_REPLICATION_V1 was a valid negative at a fixed 12-task future horizon: after charging K definition + applicability + one scope revision, only 8/100 fresh seeds had lower DEVELOPMENT description cost than complete-memory reconstruction; population DEVELOPMENT cost was 804 AST nodes higher.

No accounting term from that protocol changes here.

## Question

Is the negative a permanent displaced-complexity result, or an amortization-horizon effect? Under a fresh population, does the same fully charged installed capability cross a prospectively frozen break-even curve as independently selected future tasks accumulate?

## Fresh population

Use seeds `METALOGIC_DEVELOPMENTAL_LOOP_CAPSTONE_V1:300` through `:399`, disjoint from all prior :000..:299 batches.

Use unchanged L0, generic U, verifier-driven K acquisition, admission, and cost ledger.

## Future stream

For each seed enumerate installed-language and ordinary-U semantics through AST size 11. Jointly eligible future semantics must:
- be outside exact Cl(L0),
- differ from K,
- be reachable by both representations by size <=11.

Rank them once by SHA-256 of `seed:FUTURE:function_id`. The future sequence is the first 64 distinct jointly reachable semantics. A seed with fewer than 64 is ineligible and counts against H1; do not substitute.

## Cost ledger — unchanged

MEMORY at horizon n: sum of minimum ordinary-U AST sizes for the first n future tasks.

DEVELOPMENT at horizon n:
- K minimum ordinary-U definition size,
- +1 node initial ANY applicability hypothesis,
- +3 nodes later `context == 0` scope revision,
- +sum of minimum installed-language AST sizes for the first n future tasks.

Horizons are frozen to n in {1,2,4,8,12,16,24,32,48,64}.

For each seed record the first frozen horizon at which D < M, if any.

## Gates

H1 — at least 90/100 fresh seeds are eligible for 64 tasks.
H2 — at horizon 64, at least 60/100 total seeds have D < M.
H3 — at horizon 64, population sum(D) < sum(M) across eligible seeds.
H4 — median D/M at horizon 64 across eligible seeds is < 0.95.
H5 — among seeds that ever break even by horizon 64, the median first break-even horizon is <=32.
H6 — core acquisition + causal remove/restore gates hold for all eligible seeds.

Verdict `PASS_GOVERNED_AMORTIZATION_V1` iff H1-H6 all pass. Otherwise record `VALID_NEGATIVE_GOVERNED_AMORTIZATION_V1`.

No horizon extension beyond 64, cost-rule change, seed replacement, or target substitution is allowed after execution.

## Claim boundary

A pass would show only that in this exact finite world the cost of a verified, scoped, revisable installed capability can amortize over a sufficiently long mechanically selected future task stream under a frozen AST-node accounting. It is not wall-clock economics, natural-code economics, encoding-independent compression, or universal benefit.
