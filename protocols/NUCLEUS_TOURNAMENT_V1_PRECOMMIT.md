# NUCLEUS TOURNAMENT V1 — Sealed Recursive Third Microcosm

Status: **FROZEN BEFORE OUTCOME IMPLEMENTATION OR EXECUTION**

## Question

Does a three-operation developmental nucleus

[
N = {	extbf{Differentiate},	extbf{Mediate},	extbf{Integrate}}
]

suffice to regenerate progressively deeper executable structure in a sealed finite world, and are all three roles causally necessary under matched resource limits?

The experiment is intentionally not a stack-design exercise. The only privileged developmental roles are the three candidate nuclear operations. Everything else must be either external authority or structure generated during the run.

## Interpretive mapping

The executable roles are:

1. **DIFFERENTIATE (D)**  
   From an exact residual, identify the least currently available distinction set on which the protected consequence becomes a well-defined function.

2. **MEDIATE (M)**  
   Construct the finite relation induced on that distinction set. No named Boolean operator is supplied to the learner; the mediator is compiled extensionally from verified consequence.

3. **INTEGRATE (I)**  
   Install a verified mediator as a unit-cost executable feature with provenance and dependencies so it can become a parent of later development. Integration also owns targeted removal/restoration of installed descendants during causal ablation.

External authority is held fixed for every arm and is **not** counted as a candidate internal primitive. An OPEN authority packet may not promote any structure.

## Recursive interpretation

Recursion is not supplied as a fourth operator.

It is present only if an integrated child from generation t changes the representational frontier from which generation t+1 is constructed:

[
C_t in 	ext{Parents}(C_{t+1}).
]

The decisive test is therefore child-becomes-parent recursion rather than repeated execution of an unchanged loop.

## Sealed microcosm

### Carrier

Each world uses the full 32-row carrier of five opaque binary coordinates.

The learner receives opaque feature identities only. Human names such as AND, XOR, memory, state, object, or relation are never supplied.

### Hidden relation family

The authority constructs each developmental target from a binary truth table that depends essentially on both arguments. Candidate relations are represented only by their four output bits.

For each episode the relation is selected deterministically by SHA-256 from the frozen world seed, subject only to the preregistered admissibility tests below.

### Golden-child chain

A six-generation chain is constructed mechanically:

- G1 uses two primitive opaque coordinates.
- G2 uses the verified child from G1 and a fresh primitive coordinate.
- G3 uses the verified child from G2 and a fresh primitive coordinate.
- ...
- G6 uses the verified child from G5 and another primitive coordinate.

For every generation after G1, the world generator must certify before evaluation that:

1. the target is novel relative to the current primitive feature set;
2. both declared parents are essential;
3. the target is not functionally determined by any pair of original primitive coordinates alone;
4. the declared previous child participates in at least one minimum sufficient parent pair.

If a hashed relation fails these structural conditions, the generator advances deterministically to the next hashed relation. This is world construction, not outcome rescue.

### OPEN authority control

Before G1, the system receives one deliberately incomplete positive-evidence episode.

The correct route is `UNKNOWN`.

No candidate arm may install, remove, or revise structure from this episode.

## Candidate nuclei

The tournament executes every subset of the three roles:

- NONE
- D
- M
- I
- DM
- DI
- MI
- DMI

All arms receive the identical world, authority, verifier, target order, budgets, and primitive features.

Two additional controls are frozen:

- **DMI_COLD** — full DMI machinery, but installed developmental state is deleted before every closed episode.
- **ORACLE_STACK** — receives the true two-parent identities from the hidden generator and may compile/install the verified relation. This is an engineering upper-bound reference only, not a nucleus candidate.

## Matched search rules

### D present

The arm may exhaustively test current feature subsets in increasing cardinality and stable ID order to locate a minimum sufficient pair. Each tested subset is charged one distinction test.

A pair is sufficient only if the protected consequence is a deterministic function of its joint values on the complete carrier and both inputs are essential.

### D absent, M present

The mediator may search blindly over all currently available unordered feature pairs and all 16 binary extensional truth tables in a deterministic SHA-ordered proposal sequence.

Each candidate sent to the authority is charged one verifier candidate check.

This ensures removing D does not make construction logically impossible by definition; it converts directed differentiation into blind proposal search.

### M absent

No novel relation may be constructed. Existing installed/primitive features may still be reused.

### I absent

A verified mediator may discharge the current episode transiently but is not added to the next episode's feature language.

No hidden answer or target-specific expression may be stored as an executable feature.

## Resource boundary

Per closed episode:

- blind mediator proposal budget without D: **48 verifier candidate checks**;
- directed D pair tests: no more than the complete pair frontier of current features;
- one mediator may be installed per episode;
- no arm may recursively construct multiple hidden intermediates inside a single episode.

The one-child-per-episode rule is essential: later reach must come from prior developmental capital rather than from reconstructing the entire historical chain inside one target episode.

## Causal child ablation

After the headline DMI arm completes G4:

1. remove exactly the G3 child and its descendants;
2. re-run the frozen G4 target under the identical per-episode rules;
3. G4 must become unreachable;
4. restore exactly the sealed G3 child and dependencies;
5. G4 must again verify.

No other state may change during remove/restore.

## Counterevidence / metamorphosis control

After G6, authority supplies a verified replacement consequence for the relation that produced G3.

The full DMI arm must:

1. revoke the obsolete G3 child;
2. remove every installed descendant that causally depends on it;
3. preserve unaffected ancestors;
4. construct and verify the replacement G3' under the same three operations;
5. regenerate G4'–G6' prospectively from the changed lineage.

This is a test of continuity through transformation, not preservation of obsolete form.

The replacement relation is selected mechanically from the same essential binary relation family before the result is known.

## Replication sweep

The headline world uses seed:

After the headline result, execute 100 deterministic worlds:

`TRISKELION_NUCLEUS_TOURNAMENT_V1_SWEEP:000` through `:099`.

No gate, budget, role definition, relation family, chain depth, or candidate arm may change during the sweep.

- distinction tests;

Primary scientific quantity:

[
operatorname{RegenerativeReach}(N)
]

= deepest recursively generated verified lineage under the frozen episode/resource protocol.

Secondary economics compare full DMI with DMI_COLD and ORACLE_STACK using typed counts; no silent scalarization of distinction tests and verifier candidate checks.

## Golden-child criterion

A generated child C counts only if all hold:

1. **Novelty** — C was not an active feature before the episode.
2. **Dual dependence** — both selected parents are essential.
3. **Resolution** — exact authority verifies C on the complete carrier.
4. **Integration** — when I is present, C enters the executable feature language with provenance/dependencies.
5. **Generativity** — a later verified child depends on C.
6. **Causal necessity** — targeted ablation of C removes the protected descendant reach and exact restoration returns it.

## Headline gates

H1. OPEN evidence yields UNKNOWN and zero state mutation for every arm.

H2. DMI verifies G1–G6.

H3. Every DMI generated child satisfies novelty, dual dependence, exact verification, and provenance requirements.

H4. G2–G6 each use the immediately previous verified child as a parent.

H5. Targeted G3 ablation removes G4 reach; exact G3 restore returns G4 reach.

H6. DMI_COLD cannot reproduce the full six-generation lineage under the one-child-per-episode boundary.

H7. Removing I reduces recursive reach relative to DMI.

H8. Removing M reduces recursive reach relative to DMI.

H9. Removing D either reduces recursive reach or strictly increases matched verifier candidate work on the headline world; D is not declared logically necessary if blind MI achieves equal reach within budget.

H10. Counterevidence revokes G3 and descendants while preserving unaffected ancestors.

H11. DMI regenerates G3'–G6' after the frozen counterevidence change.

H12. No proper subset of DMI matches full DMI on all of: six-generation reach, causal child ablation, and post-counterevidence regeneration.

## Sweep classification

Record exact rates for every candidate arm.

A strong triadic signal requires:

- DMI full six-generation pre-change reach in at least 95/100 sweep worlds;
- DMI post-counterevidence regeneration through G6' in at least 95/100;
- DMI_COLD full six-generation reach in at most 5/100;
- every single-role ablation (DM, DI, MI) loses either regenerative reach or a preregistered causal criterion in at least 90/100 worlds.

If DMI succeeds but one role is dispensable under these worlds, report a **REDUCED_NUCLEUS_SIGNAL** rather than changing the protocol.

If DMI itself fails the headline world, report a valid negative.

## Verdicts

- `PASS_TRIADIC_NUCLEUS_SIGNAL_V1` — H1–H12 pass and strong sweep criteria pass.
- `REDUCED_NUCLEUS_SIGNAL_V1` — DMI passes the developmental headline, but at least one role is not shown necessary by the frozen tournament.
- `PARTIAL_NUCLEUS_SIGNAL_V1` — some but not all DMI headline developmental gates pass.
- `VALID_NEGATIVE_NUCLEUS_TOURNAMENT_V1` — DMI fails to regenerate the preregistered lineage.
- Infrastructure failure remains null and is not a scientific verdict.

## Claim boundary

Even a full PASS would establish only a bounded exact finite-world causal result for this three-role developmental nucleus under the supplied opaque carrier, finite binary relation meta-language, exact external authority, one-child-per-episode boundary, and fixed resources.

It would **not** establish that three is metaphysically fundamental, that these labels are uniquely correct, that natural intelligence has exactly three primitives, that all developmental stacks must emerge from DMI, or that the nucleus is sufficient in open-ended natural worlds.

The scientific purpose is narrower:

> test whether a minimal recursive third-making cycle can regenerate executable developmental structure, survive targeted ablation and counterevidence, and causally compound across generations without being handed a domain-specific stack.
