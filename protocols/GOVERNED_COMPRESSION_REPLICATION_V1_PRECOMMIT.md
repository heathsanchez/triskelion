## Question

Does installing a verified reusable capability reduce total future description/search cost after charging the capability definition, applicability rule, and one later scope revision, rather than merely moving complexity into persistent structure?

This is a fresh prospective response to the encoding/displaced-complexity residual recorded in V102. It does not reuse V102's three positive cases or seeds used by the previous capstone/memory tests.

## Future task stream

After K is independently verified and sealed, enumerate the installed language through syntax size 9. Mechanically rank all semantics outside exact Cl(L0), excluding K, by SHA-256 of `seed:FUTURE:function_id`. Select the first 12 distinct semantics that are reachable both:

- with installed K by size <=9; and
- in ordinary U by size <=9.

If fewer than 12 jointly reachable semantics exist, the episode is ineligible and counts against the PASS threshold; do not replace it with another seed.

This joint-reachability restriction deliberately prevents right-censoring from giving DEVELOPMENT free credit.

## Cost ledger

Use AST-node count only, frozen before outcomes.

MEMORY / reconstruct-each-time cost:
- no persistent K charge;
- for each future target, charge its minimum ordinary-U expression size through size 9.

DEVELOPMENT cost:
- one-time K definition charge = K's minimum ordinary-U expression size;
- one-time initial applicability hypothesis charge = 1 node (`ANY`);
- one later counterexample-driven scope revision charge = 3 nodes (`context == 0`);
- for each future target, charge its minimum installed-language expression size through size 9.

Thus DEVELOPMENT receives no free primitive: the full source definition plus a governance/scope lifecycle charge of four nodes is paid before reuse savings count.

No provenance/database byte accounting is claimed; this is a frozen structural description-cost metric.

## Gates

For each eligible episode compute total MEMORY cost M and governed DEVELOPMENT cost D over the 12-task stream.

Primary preregistered hypotheses:

H1 — at least 90/100 seeds are eligible.
H2 — at least 60/100 total seeds have D < M.
H3 — median D/M across eligible seeds is < 0.90.
H4 — the cumulative population saving `sum(M)-sum(D)` across eligible seeds is positive.
H5 — acquisition/ablation core gates remain valid on all eligible episodes.

`PASS_GOVERNED_COMPRESSION_REPLICATION_V1` iff H1-H5 all pass. Otherwise record `VALID_NEGATIVE_GOVERNED_COMPRESSION_REPLICATION_V1` without changing stream length, cost encoding, or seed set.

## Claim boundary

A pass would close only a bounded finite-world displaced-complexity objection under a prospectively frozen AST-node cost metric: after paying definition + scope + revision costs, reusable installed structure can lower cumulative future program-description cost. It does not establish encoding-independent Kolmogorov compression, wall-clock economics, natural-code savings, or universal benefit.
