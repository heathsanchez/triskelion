## Frozen candidate coordinate family

At one strict binary comparison site, candidate transformation templates are the three non-identity invertible combinations generated by:

1. `SWAP_ONLY`: swap operands and keep comparator label (`a < b -> b < a`, `a > b -> b > a`).
2. `FLIP_ONLY`: keep operands and swap strict comparator label (`a < b -> a > b`, `a > b -> a < b`).
3. `SWAP_AND_FLIP`: swap operands and swap strict comparator label (`a < b -> b > a`, `a > b -> b < a`).

All three templates are syntactically invertible. No template is privileged by name or score.

Identity is excluded because it provides no coordinate change and would trivially pass.

No non-invertible transformation is allowed into the identity candidate family.

## Discovery score

Within each leave-one-program-out fold:

- acquisition programs are all qualified programs except the held-out program;
- for every qualified acquisition site, apply each candidate template to the **unmutated correct program** at that site;
- run the unchanged upstream tests;
- score each template by exact count of verifier-preserving acquisition sites.

Selection rule is frozen:

1. maximize verifier-preserving site count;
2. require score == total acquisition sites;
3. require the winner to be unique.

If no unique perfect winner exists, that fold is `NO_DISCOVERY` and fails. No tie-breaking by expected semantics or held-out results is permitted.

## Repair transport

Acquisition capability is observed only in the LT presentation as literal `LE_TO_LT`.

After discovering a coordinate template T, derive the held-out repair by conjugating the acquisition repair through T at the comparator-label/operand-coordinate level.

For the frozen candidate family:
- if T maps LT presentation to GT presentation, conjugation yields `GE_TO_GT`;
- otherwise no usable transported GT repair exists.

The held-out program is not used during selection.

## Evaluation

## Frozen gates

### G1 — natural qualification
At least 8 qualified causal tasks across at least 4 distinct programs.

### G2 — fold coverage
Every qualified program is held out exactly once; no program leaks into its acquisition fold.

### G3 — relation discovery
Every fold has a unique perfect verifier-preserving transformation selected from the three-candidate family.

### G4 — relation consistency
The independently selected relation is the same template across all folds. This is evaluated only after fold-local selection; it cannot be used to break ties.

### G5 — literal baseline failure
Literal `LE_TO_LT` solves 0 held-out GT tasks.

### G6 — discovered quotient transfer
The acquisition-only discovered relation transports the repair and solves 100% of held-out GT tasks.

### G7 — causal ablation
Removing the transported repair restores failure on 100% of held-out tasks.

### G8 — held-out independence
No held-out program's test result contributes to relation selection in its fold.

### G9 — negative controls
Report verifier-preservation scores for all three candidate templates in every fold. At least one losing candidate must fail on at least one acquisition site in every fold; otherwise the verifier does not identify the relation.
