# E0006 — enlarge K tile

Status: precommitted before intervention benchmark.

## Observation / residual

E0004 (BM 64→128) reduced the exposed 4096x5120x4096 A100 mean from 1392 us to 1075 us. E0005 then changed only BN 64→128 and reduced it again to 791 us while passing the benchmark correctness gate. Relative to E0003, the same algorithm is now 43.2% faster. Tile geometry / operand reuse is therefore still a live residual.

## Hypothesis

With the output tile now 128x128, BK=32 may impose avoidable loop/control and pipeline overhead across K=4096. Doubling only BK from 32 to 64 should reduce K-loop iterations while preserving the successful 128x128 output tile.

## Strongest rival

A larger K tile may increase register/shared-memory pressure or reduce pipeline effectiveness enough to offset the lower loop count.

## Smallest separating intervention

Change BK from 32 to 64 only. Keep BM=128, BN=128, num_warps=4, num_stages=3, program mapping, arithmetic, and output type unchanged.

## Prospective prediction

Benchmark correctness passes. Exposed 4096x5120x4096 mean runtime improves by at least 3% from E0005's 791 us baseline.

## Kill criterion

Kill if compilation/correctness fails or mean runtime improvement is <=1%.

## Branding gate

Ranked mode remains mechanically blocked until the external entrant identity is verified to visibly contain `MathGraph`.
