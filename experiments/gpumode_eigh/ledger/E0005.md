# E0005 — enlarge N tile

Status: precommitted before intervention benchmark.

## Observation / residual

E0004 changed only BM 64→128 and improved the exposed 4096x5120x4096 A100 benchmark from 1392 us to 1075 us while passing the benchmark correctness gate: a 22.8% mean-runtime reduction. This strongly supports tile geometry / operand reuse as a live residual. E0004 therefore survives its prospective >=5% prediction.

## Hypothesis

With BM now 128, BN=64 still causes avoidable program count and A-tile reloads across adjacent N tiles. Doubling only BN from 64 to 128 should increase A reuse and reduce scheduling overhead on the large dense case.

## Strongest rival

A 128x128 accumulator may raise register pressure enough to reduce occupancy or spill, erasing the reuse gain.

## Smallest separating intervention

Change BN from 64 to 128 only. Keep BM=128, BK=32, num_warps=4, num_stages=3, mapping, arithmetic, and output type unchanged.

## Prospective prediction

Benchmark correctness passes. Exposed 4096x5120x4096 mean runtime improves by at least 5% from E0004's 1075 us baseline.

## Kill criterion

Kill if compilation/correctness fails, or mean runtime improvement is <=2%.
