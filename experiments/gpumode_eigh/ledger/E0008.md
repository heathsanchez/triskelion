# E0008 — increase pipeline stages

Status: precommitted before intervention benchmark.

## Observation / residual

The promoted E0005 configuration is BM=128, BN=128, BK=32, num_warps=4, num_stages=3 at 791 us. E0007 changed only num_stages 3→2 and regressed sharply to 1000 us while preserving correctness. Therefore stage 3 is not excess pressure; it is providing substantial latency hiding on this large dense case.

## Hypothesis

If the kernel remains latency-sensitive at num_stages=3, increasing only num_stages from 3 to 4 may hide additional memory/compute latency and reduce mean runtime without changing tile geometry or arithmetic.

## Strongest rival

Stage 3 may already be the optimum; stage 4 may add shared-memory/register pressure or scheduling overhead without enough extra overlap, causing no gain or a regression.

## Smallest separating intervention

From the promoted E0005 baseline BM=128, BN=128, BK=32, num_warps=4, change only num_stages from 3 to 4. Keep mapping, arithmetic, output type, and benchmark mode unchanged.

## Prospective prediction

Benchmark correctness passes. Exposed 4096x5120x4096 mean runtime improves by at least 2% from 791 us (target <=775 us).

## Kill criterion

Kill if compilation/correctness fails or mean runtime improvement is <=0.5% (runtime >=787 us). Values between 775 and 787 us are weak/ambiguous and require replication before promotion.
