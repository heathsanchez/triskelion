# E0007 — reduce pipeline stages

Status: KILLED by official benchmark.

## Observation / residual

E0005's promoted geometry is BM=128, BN=128, BK=32, num_warps=4, num_stages=3 and runs the exposed 4096x5120x4096 A100 case in 791 us. E0006 changed only BK 32→64 and regressed to 838 us despite halving K-loop iterations. The larger K tile therefore increased resource/pipeline cost enough to dominate its loop-overhead benefit.

## Hypothesis

At the successful 128x128x32 geometry, num_stages=3 may retain more in-flight tile state than this kernel needs. Reducing only num_stages from 3 to 2 may lower shared-memory/register pressure and improve occupancy while preserving the successful output and K tiles.

## Strongest rival

The kernel may be latency-bound enough that the third stage is hiding memory/compute latency; reducing to 2 stages could expose pipeline bubbles and regress runtime.

## Smallest separating intervention

Restore the promoted E0005 tile geometry BM=128, BN=128, BK=32 and change only num_stages from 3 to 2 relative to that promoted baseline. Keep num_warps=4, mapping, arithmetic, output type, benchmark mode, and all other parameters unchanged.

## Prospective prediction

Benchmark correctness passes. Exposed 4096x5120x4096 mean runtime improves by at least 2% from E0005's 791 us baseline (target <=775 us).

## Kill criterion

Kill if compilation/correctness fails or mean runtime improvement is <=0.5% (runtime >=787 us). Values between 775 and 787 us are weak/ambiguous and do not earn promotion without a replicate.

## Official result

GPU MODE submission 936291, matmul_v2 A100, benchmark mode: correctness passed; runtime = 1000 ± 0.6 us (fast 999 us, slow 1001 us).

Relative to the promoted E0005 baseline of 791 us, E0007 is 209 us slower, a 26.4% regression.

Verdict: KILL E0007. Restore num_stages=3.

Interpretation: the third pipeline stage is materially useful; the residual is not excess stage-state pressure at stage 3. This licenses the symmetric test of whether additional latency hiding at stage 4 improves the same frozen tile geometry.
