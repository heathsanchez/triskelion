# E0012 — continue N-tile enlargement

Status: precommitted; not yet submitted to the hosted evaluator.

## Observation / residual

E0004 changed BM 64→128 and improved the exposed 4096x5120x4096 A100 benchmark from 1392 us to 1075 us. E0005 then changed BN 64→128 and improved again to 791 us. Subsequent one-variable probes all failed: BK 32→64 = 838 us; stages 3→2 = 1000 us; stages 3→4 = 843 us; warps 4→8 = 828 us; warps 4→2 = 2.91 ms; grouped-M mapping with GROUP_M=8 = 869 us. Thus the strongest surviving causal signal is still output-tile enlargement / operand reuse, particularly the successful BN continuation.

## Hypothesis

At BM=128, increasing BN from 128 to 256 may further reduce program count across N=5120 and increase reuse of each loaded A tile enough to offset the larger accumulator footprint.

## Strongest rival

A 128x256 accumulator may create excessive register pressure, reduce occupancy, spill, or fail compilation, overwhelming the extra A reuse.

## Smallest separating intervention

From the promoted E0005 baseline, change only BN from 128 to 256. Keep BM=128, BK=32, num_warps=4, num_stages=3, row-major mapping, arithmetic, output type, and benchmark mode unchanged.

## Prospective prediction

Benchmark correctness passes. Exposed 4096x5120x4096 mean runtime improves by at least 3% from E0005's 791 us baseline (target <=767 us).

## Kill criterion

Kill if compilation/correctness fails or mean runtime improvement is <=1% (runtime >=783 us). Values from 768 to 782 us are weak/ambiguous and require replication before promotion.
