# E0004 — enlarge M tile

Status: precommitted before intervention benchmark.

## Observation / residual

The first accepted A100 `matmul_v2` Triton candidate passed all 5 correctness tests. In unranked benchmark mode, the exposed 4096x5120x4096 case measured 1392 ± 1.2 us (best 1388 us, worst 1396 us). The current kernel uses a 64x64x32 tile with 4 warps.

## Hypothesis

For the large dense case, the 64-row output tile leaves avoidable program-count and B-tile reload overhead. Doubling only the M tile from 64 to 128 should increase reuse of each loaded B tile and reduce scheduling overhead without changing the algorithm or K blocking.

## Strongest rival

The kernel may already be limited by register pressure / occupancy or tensor-core throughput, in which case enlarging M will be neutral or slower.

## Smallest separating intervention

Change BM from 64 to 128 only. Keep BN=64, BK=32, num_warps=4, num_stages=3, mapping, arithmetic, and output type unchanged.

## Prospective prediction

Correctness remains 5/5. On the exposed 4096x5120x4096 benchmark, mean runtime improves by at least 5% from the 1392 us E0003 baseline.

## Kill criterion

Kill the hypothesis if correctness fails, compilation fails, or exposed large-case mean improvement is <=2% (including regression).
