# E0011 — grouped M program ordering for L2 reuse

Status: KILLED by official benchmark.

## Observation / residual

The promoted E0005 kernel is BM=128, BN=128, BK=32, num_warps=4, num_stages=3 at 791 us. Adjacent launch parameters are now bracketed and worse: stages 2/4 and warps 2/8 all regress. The remaining obvious residual is that program IDs are traversed in simple row-major order, so neighboring programs sweep N before advancing M. That ordering can reload A tiles across distant scheduling windows and underuse L2 locality.

## Hypothesis

Grouping consecutive program IDs across a small block of M tiles before advancing through N will improve L2 reuse of A/B tiles and reduce runtime without changing mathematical work, tile sizes, warps, stages, or arithmetic.

## Strongest rival

The current row-major ordering may already schedule well enough on this single large shape, while grouped ordering may add index overhead or reduce beneficial B-tile locality and produce no measurable gain.

## Smallest separating intervention

Restore the full promoted E0005 configuration BM=128, BN=128, BK=32, num_warps=4, num_stages=3. Change only program-ID mapping from row-major to the standard grouped-M traversal with GROUP_M=8. Keep grid size, arithmetic, masks, output type, and benchmark mode unchanged.

## Prospective prediction

Benchmark correctness passes. Exposed 4096x5120x4096 mean runtime improves by at least 3% from E0005's 791 us baseline (target <=767 us).

## Kill criterion

Kill if compilation/correctness fails or mean runtime improvement is <=1% (runtime >=783 us). Values between 768 and 782 us are weak/ambiguous and require replication before promotion.

## Official result

GPU MODE submission 936297, matmul_v2 A100, benchmark mode: correctness passed; runtime = 869 ± 0.3 us (fast 868 us, slow 869 us).

Relative to E0005's 791 us promoted baseline, E0011 is 78 us slower, a 9.86% regression.

Verdict: KILL E0011. Row-major mapping remains preferred over GROUP_M=8 on the exposed shape.

Interpretation: generic grouped-M L2 ordering does not improve this already-large 128x128 tile on this workload. The strongest surviving structural signal remains larger output-tile reuse itself, especially the successful BN 64→128 move. A further BN continuation is therefore more licensed than additional launch or mapping knob search.
